# Music

Drop background tracks here (or into a project's own folder). Audio files in this
folder are git-ignored, so licensed music never ends up on GitHub.

Free sources (check each track's license — most below allow use in social videos):

- **YouTube Audio Library** — studio.youtube.com → Audio Library. Free for YouTube; many tracks need no attribution.
- **Pixabay Music** — pixabay.com/music. Free for commercial use, no attribution required.
- **Free Music Archive** — freemusicarchive.org. Filter by Creative Commons license (CC BY needs credit in the caption).
- **Incompetech (Kevin MacLeod)** — incompetech.com. CC BY: credit "Music by Kevin MacLeod".

Tips for talking-head reels: pick instrumental tracks (no vocals) with a steady
tempo. The editor loops, fades, and ducks the track under your voice automatically
(`plan.json` → `audio.music`).

`_test_pad.wav` is a synthesised placeholder used for testing only.
