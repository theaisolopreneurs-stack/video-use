                      How to use the Review Studio

                      StartThe 3 stepsClaude's planTimeline Style tabsTalking to ClaudeExportTips

                      What this is

                      Claude edits your video; you direct. This page shows what Claude did and why, lets you change anything yourself, and lets you leave feedback on any single piece: a scene, a transition, a caption, a zoom, a sound. Nothing here leaves your computer.

                      The loop: watch → click what you'd change → type feedback (or change it) → Save → tell Claude “check my notes” → repeat.

                      The 3 steps (top bar)

                      1. Review & give notes: look at Claude's plan and the timeline, give feedback, change things, Save.
                      2. Watch the preview: click Make preview for a quick draft with the real cuts, captions, effects and sound. After that it updates by itself after every change (only what changed is redone, so it takes seconds) and swaps in at the same moment.
                      3. Export: when it looks right, click Looks good (twice, so it can't happen by accident), then export.

                      The orange button in the top bar is always the next step. Changing something after that takes you back to step 1, so the video always matches what you saw.

                      Claude's plan (left)

                      How Claude is directing the video: the big choices (captions, look, title, music, voice, effects) and every scene with the reason it's in. Click a choice to change it, or a scene to jump to it. Scenes is the detailed list, including what was cut (crossed out) with Restore buttons, and switches to drop or reorder scenes.

                      Timeline (bottom)

                      • Like CapCut: rows for video, captions, text, effects & zooms, overlays and audio, each piece where it plays.
                      • Click any piece to open it in ✎ Selected (right): see why it's there, change it, delete it, or write feedback for Claude. Pieces with feedback show 💬.
                      • The ◆ diamonds between scenes are transitions; orange ones have an effect, hollow ones are plain cuts. Click one to pick a transition or comment on it.
                      • Drag text, zooms, effects, stickers and the title to move them; drag their edges to make them longer or shorter. They snap to the playhead and scene edges.
                      • Drag a scene to reorder it; drag a scene's edges to trim its start or end.
                      • ✂ Split (Ctrl+B) cuts the scene at the playhead in two. ⧉ Duplicate (Ctrl+D), 🗑 Delete (Delete key), ↶ Undo (Ctrl+Z) and ↷ Redo (Ctrl+Y) work like any editor. Ctrl+S saves.
                      • Bigger video: the buttons above the video hide the Plan, Timeline or Tools. ⛶ Big video (or the F key) hides everything; F or Esc brings it all back.
                      • Click the time ruler (or any empty spot) to move the playhead. + / − / Fit zoom the timeline. Space plays and pauses.
                      • 💬 Note here pins a note to an exact time (red pins on the ruler). The round ▶ on the timeline plays and pauses. Drag the edges between panels to make the video bigger (double-click to reset).

                      Style tabs (right)

                      Captions
                      Font, colour, size and letter case, then where they sit (✨ Best stays off your face and clear of the app buttons), words to pop, emoji, and 70+ styles previewed on your footage. ✏️ Fix the caption words lets you retype any line.
                      Look
                      Colour grades plus Adjust by hand sliders.
                      Title
                      Big hook text over the first seconds. “Behind me” puts the words behind you.
                      Effects
                      Effects, zooms and camera pushes, words on screen, stickers: play to the moment and click Add.
                      Audio
                      Clean up or isolate your voice, voice level, overall loudness, music, sound effects, fades.
                      Notes
                      Anything you'd rather say than click, and a list of all your feedback.

                      Talking to Claude

                      Right here: type or say (🎤) what you want in the Tell Claude box under the video, e.g. “make the hook punchier”, “add a whip transition here”, “bigger captions”. Claude sees what you've selected and where the playhead is, makes the change, and answers in a sentence. The 💬 Chat tab on the left keeps the conversation. Your changes are saved first, automatically. This uses your own Claude account: the first time, click 🔑 Connect Claude at the top right (it turns into ✓ Claude connected), approve it in the browser page that opens, and come back.

                      Or with notes: leave 💬 feedback on any piece (timeline, Claude's plan, scenes), Save, then tell Claude “check my notes” in your Claude chat.

                      Export

                      After Looks good, pick where it's going (Reels/TikTok, feed, square, YouTube). Each shape is laid out to fit and quality-checked, with a caption file and thumbnails.

                      Tips

                      • Rather talk than type? Click the 🎤 in any text box, say it, click again. It stays on this computer.
                      • See it before you pick it: transitions, effects and zooms show as small looping previews on your own video. Hover, then click the one you like.
                      • In Claude's plan, the 💬 next to each choice and scene opens a feedback box right there; ✕ drops a scene.
                      • Watch the preview with the sound on, once, start to finish, before saying it looks good.
                      • The 🎨 menu changes the editor's colours (not your video). It's on the Home screen too.
