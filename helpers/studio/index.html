                  How to use the Review Studio

                  What this is

                  Claude has drafted an edit of your clips. This page is where you see that plan, change it, and approve it before anything slow or final is rendered. Nothing here leaves your computer.

                  The loop: look → change things or leave notes → Save → tell Claude “check my notes” → repeat until it's right → approve.

                  The 4 steps (top bar)

                  1. Edit & save — change anything, then click Save. Each save keeps the previous version, so nothing is lost.
                  2. Approve plan — when the storyboard reads right. (Click twice; the first click arms it.)
                  3. Rough cut — a fast low-res draft with real cuts, captions, graphics and sound. Watch it.
                  4. Approve rough cut — unlocks the full-quality render and exports.

                  The orange button in the top bar is always the next step. Changing anything sends you back to step 1 — that's on purpose, so the video always matches what you approved.

                  Storyboard (the list of sections)

                  • Each card is a kept section: three frames, the line you say, and why Claude kept it. The coloured label (HOOK, STAKES…) is the section's job; click it to rename.
                  • Crossed-out lines between cards are cuts (pauses, “um”s, flubbed takes) with the reason. Restore puts one back.
                  • Untick a card to drop that section. ↑ ↓ reorder. ▶ plays it in the rough cut.
                  • Edit on a card: nudge where it starts/ends (±0.1s), pick a transition into it, change its speed or speed curve, add a freeze at the end, flip it, give it a slow camera move, change its volume or voice, blur or replace its background, stabilize shaky footage, or leave a note on just that section.

                  Player and timeline

                  • The coloured bar under the video is the whole edit — one block per section, sized by length. Click a block to jump there.
                  • + Note at current time pauses the video and pins a note to that exact moment (red pins on the timeline).
                  • If you see “This rough cut is from an older plan”, click Make a new one after saving.

                  Style tabs (right side)

                  Captions
                  Dozens of caption styles previewed on your footage, grouped by feel. ✨ Best picks a spot that stays off your face and clear of the apps' buttons. Or choose Top / Middle / Bottom, or Custom and click on the video. ALL CAPS, matching emoji, and “words to pop” (the words that get the highlight). ✏️ Fix the caption words lets you retype any line to correct spelling or punctuation.
                  Look
                  Colour grades grouped as Natural, Warm, Cinematic, Film, Trend and Black & white, plus Adjust by hand sliders for brightness, warmth, sharpness and more. Add your own .cube LUTs to the luts/ folder.
                  Title
                  Big hook text over the first seconds. “Behind me” puts the words behind you.
                  Effects
                  Hand swipe reveal (the video changes behind your hand as you swipe), shake, glitch, glow, kaleidoscope, comic book and many more. Play the video to the moment, pick an effect and a length, click Add. Captions and graphics stay clean on top. Add your own words on screen and stickers the same way.
                  Audio
                  Voice clean-up or Isolate voice (only you, no background), voice level, whole-video loudness, voice effects, background music (▶ Listen first), volume, “duck under voice”, a beat pulse, fades, and a sound on popped words. Put tracks in the music/ folder.
                  Notes
                  Anything you'd rather say than click: “hook is too slow”, “cut the second story”, “make it 45 seconds”.

                  Talking to Claude

                  After Save, go back to your Claude chat and say “check my notes”. Claude reads every note (overall, per section and timestamped), makes the changes, and the Latest changes box here says what it did. You can also just ask in the chat — e.g. “add a checklist graphic when I list the steps”, “use the screen recording as B-roll at 0:12”, “spell ‘derive’ correctly in the captions”.

                  Export

                  Once the rough cut is approved, tell Claude where it's going — “export for Reels, Instagram feed and YouTube”. You get each shape (9:16, 4:5, 1:1, 16:9) re-laid out and quality-checked, a caption file (.srt), chapters, and three thumbnail options, in the project's exports/ folder. Ask Claude for titles, a description and hashtags too.

                  Tips

                  • Rather talk than type? Click the 🎤 in any text box, say your note, and click it again. Your browser will ask to use the microphone the first time. What you say stays on this computer.
                  • Lead with your strongest line — a number, a result or a bold claim — even if you said it later.
                  • Less is more: one transition between topics, a zoom or graphic every 5–10 seconds, not every line.
                  • Watch the rough cut with the sound on, once, start to finish, before approving.
                  • The 🎨 menu in the top bar changes this page's colour theme (it doesn't affect your video).
